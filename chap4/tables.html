Jack Ma & Alibaba Group

Jack Ma is a Chinese entrepreneur and one of the co-founders of Alibaba Group. He played an important role in the development of Alibaba from a small internet business into a major technology and e-commerce company. His journey is often associated with entrepreneurship, technology, and persistence.

About Jack Ma

Jack Ma was born on September 10, 1964, in Hangzhou, China. Before becoming an entrepreneur, he worked as an English teacher. His first exposure to the internet during a trip to the United States influenced his decision to explore opportunities in the emerging internet industry.
